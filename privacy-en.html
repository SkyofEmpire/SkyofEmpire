1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Sky of Empire collects, uses, and protects personal information when you visit our website or use our services. We take the protection of your data seriously and handle your information responsibly.

2. Data we may collect

  • Contact information such as your name and email address if you send us a message.
  • Technical data such as browser type, device type, IP address, and general usage information.
  • Messages or requests that you send to us through contact or support forms.
  • Basic preferences such as the language selected on the website.

3. How we use data

We may use collected data to operate the website, provide support, respond to inquiries, improve content and services, enhance security and technical stability, and comply with legal obligations where required.

4. Legal basis for processing

Personal data is processed where necessary to provide our services, communicate with you at your request, protect our legitimate interests in operating and improving the website, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

5. Sharing of data

We do not sell your personal data. Information may only be shared when necessary with trusted service providers supporting website operation or technical support, or when required by law or necessary to protect our rights and users.

6. Data storage and retention

We keep personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes for which it was collected or as long as required by law. After that, data will be deleted or appropriately restricted.

7. Security

We take appropriate technical and organizational measures to help protect personal data against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. However, no electronic transmission or storage method is completely secure.

8. Cookies and similar technologies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to support essential functionality and improve usability. More information can be found in our Cookie Policy.

9. Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, deletion of, restriction of processing of, or objection to the processing of your personal data. You may also have the right to lodge a complaint with a competent data protection authority.

10. External links

Our website may contain links to external websites or services. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those third parties. Please review their policies separately.

11. Children’s privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children where prohibited by law. If you believe that information relating to a child has been submitted to us without proper authorization, please contact us.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page together with the revised last updated date.

13. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or the processing of your personal data, you can contact us by email at support@skyofempire.com.
