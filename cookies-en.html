1. INTRODUCTION

This Cookie Policy explains how Sky of Empire (“we”, “us” or “our”) uses cookies and similar technologies when you use our website or digital services. It describes what information we collect, how we use it, and what rights you have.

2. WHAT ARE COOKIES?

Cookies are small text files stored on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are used to operate websites more efficiently and to provide information about user behavior.

Cookies allow us to recognize your device and remember your preferences.

3. WHAT TYPES OF COOKIES DO WE USE?

3.1 Necessary Cookies

These cookies are required for the operation of the website and cannot be disabled.

Examples:

  • Session management
  • Security and authentication
  • Storage of privacy settings

3.2 Analytics and Statistics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors use our website so that we can improve our services.

Examples:

  • Google Analytics
  • Firebase Analytics

3.3 Functional Cookies

These cookies enable enhanced functions and personalization.

Examples:

  • Saving language settings
  • User preferences

3.4 Marketing and Advertising Cookies

These cookies are used to display relevant advertising and to measure the effectiveness of campaigns.

Examples:

  • Google Ads
  • Meta (Facebook) Pixel
  • Unity Ads
  • AdMob

4. THIRD-PARTY COOKIES

We may use third-party providers that use cookies on our behalf, including:

  • Google
  • Apple
  • Unity Technologies
  • Meta Platforms
  • Firebase
  • Advertising and analytics partners

These third parties process data in accordance with their own privacy policies.

5. LEGAL BASIS

The use of cookies is based on:

  • Your consent pursuant to Art. 6 para. 1 lit. a GDPR
  • Our legitimate interests pursuant to Art. 6 para. 1 lit. f GDPR
  • Legal obligations

Necessary cookies do not require consent.

6. HOW CAN YOU MANAGE COOKIES?

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings.

Browser settings:

  • Chrome: https://support.google.com/chrome
  • Safari: https://support.apple.com
  • Firefox: https://support.mozilla.org
  • Edge: https://support.microsoft.com

Mobile devices:

  • iOS: Settings → Privacy → Tracking
  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads

Please note that disabling cookies may limit the functionality of our services.

7. RETENTION PERIOD

Cookies may either be:

  • Session cookies: deleted when the browser is closed.
  • Persistent cookies: remain stored on your device until they are deleted or expire.
