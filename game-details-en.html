Game Details

Sky of Empire is a strategy and aerial world game set in a universe of floating islands, air bases, and tactical battles. Players build their power, expand their influence, and develop their empire in the skies.

Game Content

  • Build and expand your own air base.
  • Manage resources and upgrade buildings and technologies.
  • Use aircraft and aerial units in missions and battles.
  • Explore a vast cloud world full of challenges.
  • Compete with other players and progress through power and development systems.

Progression and Development

As you advance in the game, you can unlock new buildings, improve units, develop your capabilities, and increase the strength of your empire. Success depends on planning, resource management, and making the right decisions at the right time.

Experience

The game offers an experience that combines strategy, progression, and management within a visual atmosphere inspired by skies, clouds, and flying bases. The goal is to build a powerful empire and establish your presence in this aerial world.
